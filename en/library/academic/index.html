NeurIPS Proceedings

Official NeurIPS proceedings (1 entry)

  • Stochastic Taylor Derivative Estimator: Efficient amortization for arbitrary differential operators

    Publisher: Zekun Shi、Zheyuan Hu、Min Lin、Kenji Kawaguchi · Collection: NeurIPS 2024 · Main Conference · Best Paper · Format: Research paper · Authority tier: R · Review status: Passed six-step review; NeurIPS 2024 Main Track Best Paper; not independently reproduced

    Uses a stochastic Taylor derivative estimator to efficiently contract arbitrary high-order derivative tensors of multivariate functions, making supervision with high-dimensional, high-order differential operators more feasible.

    Evidence use: For checking STDE, the computational method for supervising high-order differential operators in PINNs, and the efficiency conclusions as scoped by the paper and the committee.

    Limitations: The strongest evidence comes from PINNs and differential-operator supervision; it should not be described as a general speedup for all neural network training or backpropagation. The reported speed and memory gains depend on the specific operators, implementation, hardware and baselines; this site has not reproduced them independently.

    Related concepts: Backpropagation · Supervised Learning · LLM Inference Optimization

  • Minimalist Vision with Freeform Pixels

    Publisher: Jeremy Klotz、Shree K. Nayar · Collection: Springer Nature · ECCV 2024 · Best Paper · Format: Research paper · Authority tier: R · Review status: Passed importance review · ECCV 2024 Best Paper

    The paper proposes a minimalist vision system: the camera hardware is modeled as the first layer of a neural network, and task training jointly determines a small number of free-form physical pixels and the downstream inference network, performing vision tasks at a very small sensing scale while preserving privacy and enabling self-powered operation.

    Evidence use: For understanding how learned sensing, computational imaging, privacy-preserving vision and edge inference are co-designed across hardware and models.

    Limitations: The Best Paper award shows an outstanding contribution under the ECCV 2024 award process; it does not mean all of the paper's conclusions are permanently correct or apply to every vision task. This site verified the official award, the award citation, the Springer DOI and the public paper materials; experiments were not independently reproduced. The Springer page may offer only an abstract preview; this site shows metadata, review conclusions and the original link only, and does not copy restricted full text.
