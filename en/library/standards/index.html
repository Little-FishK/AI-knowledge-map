International AI Safety Report

Evidence review chaired by Bengio and endorsed by experts from many countries (1 entry)

  • International AI Safety Report 2026

    Publisher: International AI Safety Report · Collection: International AI Safety Report · Format: International scientific evidence assessment · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    An international team of experts synthesizes the scientific evidence on general-purpose AI capabilities, risks and risk management, without making specific policy recommendations.

    Evidence use: Supports the international evidence consensus, disagreements and uncertainties as of the report cutoff.

    Limitations: A scientific assessment, not a normative policy document. Conclusions must be tied to the 2026 evidence cutoff.
