  • EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)

    Publisher: European Parliament and Council of the European Union · Collection: EU AI Act · Format: EU regulation · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Establishes uniform EU rules on risk tiers, prohibited practices, high-risk systems, general-purpose AI models and market surveillance.

    Evidence use: Supports the Act's official text, who it applies to, its risk classification and obligations that apply in phases.

    Limitations: Specific provisions apply in phases. Also check later amendments, implementing acts and guidelines.

    Related concepts: AI Governance and Regulation · Model Evaluation and Benchmarks · Training Data Governance

  • Guidelines on prohibited AI practices under the EU AI Act

    Publisher: European Commission · Collection: EU AI Act Guidelines · Format: Regulatory interpretation guidance · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Explains the scope of practices prohibited by the AI Act, with examples, to help parties identify the boundaries of Article 5.

    Evidence use: Supports the European Commission's non-binding interpretation of prohibited practices.

    Limitations: Non-binding guidelines. Final interpretation rests with the Court of Justice of the EU.

    Related concepts: AI Governance and Regulation · Bias and Fairness

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • Guidelines on the definition of an "AI system" under the EU AI Act

    Publisher: European Commission · Collection: EU AI Act Guidelines · Format: Regulatory interpretation guidance · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Explains the elements of the definition of an AI system and where it applies.

    Evidence use: Supports the European Commission's interpretation of the definition of an AI system.

    Limitations: Non-binding guidelines. Must be judged on the facts of each case.

    Related concepts: AI Governance and Regulation

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • General-Purpose AI Code of Practice

    Publisher: European Commission / EU AI Office · Collection: EU AI Act GPAI · Format: Voluntary compliance tool · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Provides a voluntary compliance path for GPAI providers to meet the AI Act's transparency, copyright and systemic risk obligations.

    Evidence use: Supports the code's commitments and chapters and how they map to AI Act obligations.

    Limitations: Signing up to the code is voluntary. Cannot replace the text of the regulation.

    Related concepts: AI Governance and Regulation · Training Data Governance · Model Evaluation and Benchmarks

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • Guidelines on the scope of obligations for providers of GPAI models under the EU AI Act

    Publisher: European Commission · Collection: EU AI Act Guidelines · Format: Regulatory interpretation guidance · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Explains when something is a GPAI model or provider, and the boundaries of model modification and systemic risk obligations.

    Evidence use: Supports the European Commission's interpretation of the scope of GPAI obligations.

    Limitations: Non-binding guidelines. Should be read alongside the latest implementing measures.
