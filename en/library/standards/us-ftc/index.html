Authority tiers in this category: A1.

  • FTC rule on impersonation of government and businesses

    Publisher: U.S. Federal Trade Commission · Collection: 16 CFR Part 461 · Format: Federal trade rule · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Prohibits impersonating governments, businesses or their officials or agents in commerce, giving a direct enforcement basis against AI-driven impersonation fraud.

    Evidence use: Supports the rule text, who it applies to and whether it is in force; AI is only one possible means.

    Limitations: Not a general rule for all AI systems. Whether conduct is unlawful depends on the specific conduct and judicial facts.

    Related concepts: AI-generated Content Detection and Watermarking · AI Governance and Regulation

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • Final order in the Rite Aid facial recognition enforcement case

    Publisher: U.S. Federal Trade Commission · Collection: FTC Matter 2023190 · Format: Enforcement decision · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    The final order against Rite Aid over misidentification, consumer harm and missing safeguards caused by facial recognition.

    Evidence use: Supports the allegations, order obligations and FTC enforcement facts on biometric AI risk in this case.

    Limitations: The order binds only the parties to the case. Cannot be generalized into a uniform rule for all companies.

    Related concepts: Bias and Fairness · Privacy and Data Compliance · AI Governance and Regulation

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • Final order in the DoNotPay "AI lawyer" advertising enforcement case

    Publisher: U.S. Federal Trade Commission · Collection: FTC DoNotPay Matter · Format: Enforcement decision · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    The final order over advertising that claimed an AI service could replace human lawyers without adequate testing.

    Evidence use: Supports the AI capability claims, testing basis and consumer protection obligations in this case.

    Limitations: Applies only to the parties and the specific representations. Not a general licensing rule for legal-services AI.

    Publisher: U.S. Federal Trade Commission · Collection: FTC Matter 242 3030 · Format: Enforcement decision · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review
