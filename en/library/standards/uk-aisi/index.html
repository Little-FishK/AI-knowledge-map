  • Inspect: the UK AISI's open-source model evaluation framework

    Publisher: UK AI Security Institute · Collection: AISI Evaluation Tools · Format: Official evaluation framework · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Provides a reproducible system of tasks, datasets, solvers, scorers and logs for building and running model evaluations.

    Evidence use: Supports Inspect's official status, components and evaluation uses.

    Limitations: Tool results depend on the task and configuration. The official release note uses a blog URL, but the subject itself is a formal open-source framework.

    Related concepts: Model Evaluation and Benchmarks · AI Red Teaming

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • UK AISI model evaluation approach

    Publisher: UK AI Security Institute · Collection: AISI Evaluation Guidance · Format: Official evaluation methodology · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Explains AISI's goals for independent technical evaluation, risk areas, methodological limits and its position of not issuing "safety certification".

    Evidence use: Supports AISI's evaluation role, scope and the limits of its conclusions.

    Limitations: AISI states explicitly that it is not a regulator. Preliminary evaluations do not constitute safety claims.

    Related concepts: Model Evaluation and Benchmarks · AI Red Teaming

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • UK AISI research agenda

    Publisher: UK AI Security Institute · Collection: AISI Research Agenda · Format: Official research and evaluation framework · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Defines priority risks and methods in cyber, chemical and biological, autonomous systems, human influence, the science of evaluations and safeguards analysis.

    Evidence use: Supports AISI's current research and evaluation priorities.

    Limitations: A research agenda for a given period, not a regulatory requirement. Sensitive methods are not fully disclosed.

    Related concepts: Model Evaluation and Benchmarks · AI Red Teaming · AI Governance and Regulation

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • Principles for frontier AI misuse safeguard evaluations

    Publisher: UK AI Security Institute · Collection: AISI Safeguard Evaluation · Format: Evaluation principles and templates · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Sets out core principles for evaluating misuse safeguards, with an accompanying template of actionable questions.

    Evidence use: Supports the recommended principles and question structure for assurance evaluations.

    Limitations: A recommended practice, not a mandatory rule. Methods will be updated as frontier capabilities develop.

    Related concepts: AI Guardrails · Model Evaluation and Benchmarks · AI Red Teaming

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • Frontier AI Trends Report

    Publisher: UK AI Security Institute · Collection: AISI Public Assessments · Format: Official evidence assessment report · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Summarizes AISI's public evidence assessment of frontier model capabilities, risks and trends in safeguards.

    Evidence use: Supports frontier trend assessments and AISI measurement results as of the report date.

    Limitations: A point-in-time assessment, not a regulatory rule. Conclusions about fast-changing models must be tied to a version.

    Related concepts: Model Evaluation and Benchmarks · Scaling Laws · AI Governance and Regulation
