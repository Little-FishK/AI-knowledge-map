    Publisher: IEEE Standards Association · Collection: IEEE 7000 Series · Format: Transparency standard · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Specifies general requirements and metrics for measuring and communicating the transparency of autonomous systems.

    Evidence use: Supports statements about transparency goals, stakeholders and assessment scope.

  • IEEE 7003-2024: algorithmic bias considerations

    Publisher: IEEE Standards Association · Collection: IEEE 7000 Series · Format: Algorithmic bias standard · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Provides a process framework for identifying, documenting, assessing and mitigating algorithmic bias.

    Evidence use: Supports IEEE's standardized approach to process controls for algorithmic bias.

    Limitations: A voluntary standard. Does not prove that an individual system is free of bias.

    Publisher: IEEE Standards Association · Collection: IEEE 7000 Series · Format: Impact assessment standard · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Gives methods for identifying, measuring and monitoring the impact of intelligent systems on human well-being.

    Evidence use: Supports the metrics and process positioning of well-being impact assessment.

    Limitations: A guidance recommended practice. Must account for the specific population and scenario.
