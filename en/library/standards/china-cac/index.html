Standards and Regulation: Cyberspace Administration of China

中文版

Standards and Regulation · Cyberspace Administration of China. Formal documents on generative AI management and filing. This source has 5 entries admitted under this category's review policy; each links directly to the original.

Authority tiers in this category: A1.

  • Provisions on the Administration of Algorithmic Recommendations for Internet Information Services

    Publisher: Cyberspace Administration of China et al. · Collection: CAC Order No. 9 · Format: Departmental rules · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Sets requirements for algorithmic recommendation services on information content, user rights, filing, security assessment and supervision.

    Evidence use: Supports current regulatory requirements for algorithmic recommendation services in China.

    Limitations: Applies only to algorithmic recommendation internet information services within mainland China. Must be read with higher-level laws and later regulations.

    Related concepts: AI Governance and Regulation

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • Provisions on the Administration of Deep Synthesis of Internet Information Services

    Publisher: Cyberspace Administration of China et al. · Collection: Provisions on the Administration of Deep Synthesis · Format: Departmental rules · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Sets requirements for deep synthesis services on data, labeling, security assessment, filing and user management.

    Evidence use: Supports current obligations and covered entities for deep synthesis services in China.

    Limitations: Limited in both geographic scope and service type. Should be applied together with the new rules on labeling AI-generated content.

    Related concepts: AI-generated Content Detection and Watermarking · Voice Cloning · AI Governance and Regulation

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • Interim Measures for the Administration of Generative AI Services

    Publisher: Cyberspace Administration of China et al. · Collection: CAC Order No. 15 · Format: Departmental rules · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Sets obligations for public-facing generative AI services on training data, content safety, personal information, labeling and service governance.

    Evidence use: Supports current regulatory requirements for generative AI services in China and the explicit exemptions.

    Limitations: Does not apply to R&D uses that do not serve the public in mainland China. Must be read with filing, security assessment and other laws.

    Related concepts: AI Governance and Regulation · Training Data Governance · Privacy and Data Compliance

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • Measures for Labeling AI-Generated and Synthetic Content

    Publisher: Cyberspace Administration of China et al. · Collection: CAC Announcement No. 2 of 2025 · Format: Departmental rules · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Sets rules for explicit and implicit labeling of AI-generated and synthetic content, verification during distribution and platform responsibilities.

    Evidence use: Supports who must label, the technical methods, effective date and platform obligations.

    Limitations: Must be read with the accompanying mandatory national standard GB 45438-2025. Specific technical requirements differ by content type.

    Related concepts: AI-generated Content Detection and Watermarking · AI Governance and Regulation

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • Interim Measures for the Administration of Anthropomorphic AI Interaction Services

    Publisher: Cyberspace Administration of China et al. · Collection: Order No. 21 of the CAC and seven other departments · Format: Departmental rules · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Regulates AI services with anthropomorphic expression and interaction, covering identity disclosure, addiction risk, protection of minors and content governance.

    Evidence use: Supports current Chinese regulatory obligations for anthropomorphic AI interaction services.

    Limitations: Applies only to anthropomorphic interactive services as defined in the measures. Applies together with existing rules such as those on generative AI.

    Related concepts: AI Governance and Regulation · Human-in-the-loop (HITL)

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24
