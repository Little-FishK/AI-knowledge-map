  • GB 45438-2025: methods for labeling AI-generated and synthetic content

    Publisher: National Cybersecurity Standardization Technical Committee · Collection: National cybersecurity technology standard · Format: Mandatory national standard · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Specifies explicit and implicit labeling methods for AI-generated and synthetic text, images, audio, video and virtual scenes.

    Evidence use: Supports labeling fields, technical methods and covered entities.

    Limitations: The technical standard should be read together with the labeling measures. Applies only to the specified labeling of AI-generated content.
