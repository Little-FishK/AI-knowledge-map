  • NIST AI 800-2 IPD: practices for automated benchmark evaluation of language models

    Publisher: NIST Center for AI Standards and Innovation · Collection: NIST AI 800 Series · Format: Initial public draft · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review · Draft

    Proposes preliminary best practices for automated benchmark evaluation, covering task validity, implementation, execution and reporting.

    Evidence use: Supports the evaluation practices and terminology described in the draft.

    Limitations: An Initial Public Draft, not final guidance. The public comment period has closed; the document may be revised.

    Related concepts: Model Evaluation and Benchmarks · LLM Application Evaluation

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • NIST AI 800-3: expanding the AI evaluation toolbox with statistical models

    Publisher: NIST Center for AI Standards and Innovation · Collection: NIST AI 800 Series · Format: Official measurement methodology report · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Distinguishes fixed-benchmark accuracy from generalized accuracy and uses statistical models to improve uncertainty quantification and interpretation of results.

    Evidence use: Supports NIST AI 800-3's measurement goals, models and experimental results.

    Limitations: A methodology report, not a mandatory standard. Model assumptions must match the specific evaluation design.

    Related concepts: Model Evaluation and Benchmarks · Uncertainty Calibration and Selective Prediction

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • CAISI evaluation of DeepSeek V4 Pro

    Publisher: NIST Center for AI Standards and Innovation · Collection: CAISI Model Evaluations · Format: Official model evaluation · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Publishes CAISI's multi-domain evaluation results for DeepSeek V4 Pro, with comparison methods and appendices.

    Evidence use: Supports CAISI results for this model version on the specified benchmarks, configuration and date.

    Limitations: Model-specific and time-bound. Cannot be extrapolated into safety conclusions for every deployment.

    Related concepts: Model Evaluation and Benchmarks · Reasoning Models
