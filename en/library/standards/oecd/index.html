Standards and Regulation: OECD

中文版

Standards and Regulation · OECD. AI principles, policy observatory and intergovernmental frameworks. This source has 4 entries admitted under this category's review policy; each links directly to the original.

Authority tiers in this category: A1.

  • OECD Recommendation on AI and the AI Principles

    Publisher: Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development · Collection: OECD/LEGAL/0449 · Format: Intergovernmental recommendation · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Sets out principles for trustworthy AI, including inclusive growth, human-centered values, transparency, robustness and safety, and accountability; updated in 2024.

    Evidence use: Supports the OECD AI Principles and the revised policy recommendations.

    Limitations: A recommendation does not directly become binding domestic law. Implementation follows each member's and partner's own system.

    Related concepts: AI Governance and Regulation · Human-in-the-loop (HITL)

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • OECD Framework for the Classification of AI Systems

    Publisher: Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development · Collection: OECD AI Policy Observatory · Format: Policy classification framework · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Describes AI systems along the dimensions of people and planet, economic context, data and input, the AI model, and task and output.

    Evidence use: Supports the dimensions and policy uses of the OECD classification framework.

    Limitations: A classification tool, not a compliance certification. Must be applied to the context of the specific system.

    Related concepts: AI Governance and Regulation · Model Evaluation and Benchmarks

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • OECD explanatory memorandum on the updated definition of an AI system

    Publisher: Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development · Collection: OECD AI Publications · Format: Definitions and interpretation guidance · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Explains, element by element, the OECD definition of an AI system as updated in 2023 and its boundaries.

    Evidence use: Supports the official explanation of each element of the OECD definition of AI.

    Limitations: Explains the OECD definition; it does not automatically determine definitions in other laws.

    Related concepts: AI Governance and Regulation

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24

  • OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible AI

    Publisher: Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development · Collection: OECD Responsible Business Conduct · Format: Due diligence guidance · Authority tier: A1 · Review status: Passed single-tier standards and regulation review

    Applies the OECD due diligence cycle for responsible business conduct to identifying, preventing, tracking and communicating AI impacts.

    Evidence use: Supports the OECD's recommended steps and limits for corporate AI due diligence.

    Limitations: Non-binding guidelines. Cannot replace local legal obligations.

    Related concepts: AI Governance and Regulation · Training Data Governance

    Link last checked: 2026-09-24
