Knowledge Bases and Evaluation Organizations · AI Incident Database. AI incident IDs, records and statistics. This source has 5 entries admitted under this category's review policy; each links directly to the original.

Authority tiers in this category: A2–B. Admission policy: All core resources are included. Each secondary source is admitted according to its own database, methodology, versions and leaderboard structure; there is no cross-organization scoring scheme.

  • AI Incident Database

    Publisher: Responsible AI Collaborative · Collection: AI Incident Database · Format: AI incident database · Authority tier: A2 · Review status: living

    Assigns IDs to publicly reported real-world AI harm incidents, preserves sources and provides search and classification.

    Evidence use: Supports statements about whether an AI incident has been recorded, its sources and its classification.

    Limitations: Relies on public reporting, with regional and language bias. Inclusion does not establish liability.
