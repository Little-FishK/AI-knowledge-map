Learn AI from Scratch: Free Guide for Beginners

AI Knowledge Map is a free learning resource for complete beginners. Build a broad understanding of artificial intelligence through a connected map of concepts, from machine learning and neural networks to large language models and AI agents. Clear, concept-first explanations help you understand what each idea means, how it works, and how it connects to the bigger picture. No prior AI or programming knowledge is needed to get started.
