项目地址：https://ai-knowledge-map.com/

GitHub：https://github.com/Little-FishK/AI-knowledge-map

这是我为 AI 初学者做的免费开源学习网站。很多人并不是学不会某个概念，而是不知道大模型、Transformer、Token、RAG、Agent、MCP 等词在整个 AI 领域里处于什么位置。网站把 130 个核心概念按 9 个阶段组织成一张可交互的知识地图，并用 416 条关系展示概念之间的联系。

每个概念都有独立的深入解释页，从“为什么需要它”开始，再通过具体例子说明原理；支持简体中文和英文，阅读无需注册。代码采用 MIT 许可证，项目原创文章与配图采用 CC BY 4.0。

适合零基础学习者、想补齐 AI 全局认知的开发者，以及教师作为课程导航资源使用。

**Maker first comment**

Hi Product Hunt — I built AI Knowledge Map because beginners often encounter AI as a pile of disconnected terms. They hear about tokens, Transformers, RAG, agents, or MCP, but rarely see how those ideas fit together or what to learn first.

The site turns 130 concepts into a nine-stage path and shows 416 typed relationships between them. Every concept also has an explainer that begins with the problem it solves and walks through a concrete example. It is free to read without an account and works in English and Simplified Chinese.
