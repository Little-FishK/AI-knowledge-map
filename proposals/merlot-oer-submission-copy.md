## Description

AI Knowledge Map is a free, interactive bilingual learning resource that helps beginners understand how 130 artificial intelligence concepts connect across a nine-stage learning path. Learners can search terms, follow typed relationships among concepts, read English and Chinese explainers, and track their progress from foundations through machine learning, large language models, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), AI agents, and AI safety.

The resource can support self-directed study, course orientation, concept review, and AI curriculum planning. Browsing and reading do not require an account. Some supporting software and reference resources are currently available only in Chinese.

## Learning objectives

After using this resource, learners should be able to:

1. Identify major AI concepts and organize them into a coherent learning sequence.
2. Explain relationships among foundational machine learning, large language model, RAG, agent, and AI safety concepts.
3. Choose an appropriate next topic using prerequisite and related-concept links.
4. Track progress across a self-directed AI learning plan.
